## Overview

VendorBridge is a centralized procurement and vendor management platform designed to streamline and automate the complete procurement lifecycle.

Organizations frequently face challenges such as fragmented vendor communication, manual quotation comparison, approval delays, invoice tracking issues, and limited procurement visibility. VendorBridge addresses these challenges through a unified system that manages procurement activities from vendor onboarding to invoice processing.

The platform enables organizations to improve operational efficiency, enhance transparency, and make informed procurement decisions through structured workflows and centralized data management.

---

## Problem Statement

Procurement processes in many organizations rely on manual workflows and disconnected systems, resulting in:

* Manual vendor management
* Spreadsheet-based quotation comparison
* Delayed approval processes
* Limited procurement visibility
* Inefficient invoice tracking
* Decentralized documentation

These inefficiencies increase operational costs, create delays, and reduce overall procurement effectiveness.

---

## Solution

VendorBridge provides a centralized procurement ecosystem with the following capabilities:

* Vendor Management
* Request for Quotation (RFQ) Management
* Quotation Submission and Comparison
* Multi-Level Approval Workflows
* Purchase Order Generation
* Invoice Management
* Procurement Analytics Dashboard
* Role-Based Access Control
* Real-Time Procurement Visibility
